"Your hotel is closed / overbooked" is a common taxi and auto-rickshaw scam near airports and train stations, meant to redirect travellers to a commission-paying alternative — call the hotel directly to confirm before believing a driver's claim.

Unlicensed "government tourism office" storefronts near major tourist sites often oversell tours and houseboats at inflated prices — official state tourism offices are listed on state government tourism websites, not standalone shops.

Gem and "export scheme" scams target tourists in Jaipur, Agra, and Delhi with offers to resell overpriced gems abroad for profit — there is no resale market for this, and payments are not recoverable.

Meter tampering or "the meter is broken" claims from autos and taxis are common in high-tourist areas — agreeing on a fare upfront, or using app-based cabs with fixed pricing, avoids this.
