Sharing a live location with a trusted contact for the duration of a journey is a low-effort habit that meaningfully reduces risk, especially for late-arrival travel or overnight buses and trains.

Many overnight sleeper trains in India offer separate ladies' compartments on certain routes — worth requesting when booking, where available.

Well-reviewed hostels and guesthouses with 24-hour reception are generally easier to arrive at late than budget lodges without staff present overnight — worth checking before booking.

Keeping a copy of ID and emergency contacts separate from the original (not just a photo on the same phone that could be lost) is a basic redundancy worth having before a solo trip.
