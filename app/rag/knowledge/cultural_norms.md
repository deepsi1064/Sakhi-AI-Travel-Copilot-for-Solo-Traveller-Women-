Modest dress (covering shoulders and knees) is generally expected at temples, mosques, and other religious sites across India, regardless of gender.

Public displays of affection are less common in India than in many Western countries, especially outside major cities — matching local norms tends to reduce unwanted attention.

Removing shoes before entering homes, temples, and some shops is standard practice — a shoe rack at the entrance is the usual cue.

Direct eye contact and a firm, brief "no" are culturally understood and acceptable ways to decline unwanted attention from strangers.
